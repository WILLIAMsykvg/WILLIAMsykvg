## ProofPay

A shopping helper for real Hong Kong Shopify stores. It compares prices and can fill the cart. It cannot pay, and it never sees your location, purchase history, or card.

Before money moves, your location, your bank, and the shop each sign a yes or no. The phone checks those signatures. The bank checks them again. A no can block the payment. None of the checks can approve something that should stay blocked.

- Live demo: https://hacku-local-deployment.onrender.com
- Code: [HACKU-local](https://github.com/WILLIAMsykvg/HACKU-local)
